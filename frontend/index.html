Insurance

Simple explanations of protection options.

Insurance means paying for protection against certain risks. The exact benefits, eligibility, exclusions, premium and documents depend on the policy.
🏥
Health

Health Insurance

Helps cover eligible medical expenses according to the policy terms. In simple words: it can reduce the financial burden of eligible healthcare costs.
✓What it helps with: Eligible hospital and medical expenses.
✓Who may need it: Families, older people and anyone wanting healthcare protection.
✓Before applying: Check age, coverage, exclusions and premium.
📄 Documents that may be requested
  • Identity/address proof
  • Age/date-of-birth proof
  • Medical records or reports, if required
  • Bank details for eligible claim processing, if required
🛡️
Life

Life Insurance

Provides financial protection to eligible beneficiaries if the insured person dies during the policy period, subject to policy terms.
✓What it helps with: Financial support for eligible beneficiaries.
✓Who may need it: People whose family depends on their income.
✓Before applying: Check nominee details, exclusions, premium and policy period.
📄 Documents that may be requested
  • Identity/address proof
  • Age/date-of-birth proof
  • Nominee details and relationship proof, if required
  • Income/occupation proof, depending on policy
📄

Documents

Common documents may include identity, address, age and nominee-related proof depending on the insurance type.
